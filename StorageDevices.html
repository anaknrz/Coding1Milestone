The advancment of storage devices

While not implemented, Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine design included a memory storage mechanism for holding numbers during calculations, laying the groundwork for future storage concepts. Herman Hollerith developed the punched card system for storing data, initially used for the U.S. Census. Punched cards were an early form of data storage that influenced computing in the 20th Century. Reynold B. Johnson invented the first commercial hard drive which used magnetic disksdisks to store data. This revolutionized storage by allowing random access to data instead of sequential access.
