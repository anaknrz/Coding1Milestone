The advancment of EarBuds

Nathaniel Baldwin invented the first audio headphones in his kitchen, Sony introduced lightweight portable earphones with the Walkman, revolutionizing personal audio by making earbuds practical and widely available. The advancement of eabuds into "smart devices" was driven bycoding and software innovations like Noise Cancellation, Audio Processing Algorithms, and Health and Fitness Monitoring.
