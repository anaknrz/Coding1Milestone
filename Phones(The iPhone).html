The advancment of phones (the iPhone)

Alexander Graham bell is credited with inventing the first practical telephone. Bell's invention enabled real-time voice communication over long distances, laying the foundation for modern telecommunication. Martin Cooper invented the first handheld mobile phone which allowed communication without wires, revolutionizing personal connectivity. Steve Jobs and his team at Apple revolutionized mobile phones also known as the iPhone which combined a phone, iPod, and internet communicator into a multi-touch interface and app ecosystem.
